# Government terminology for the six-finding barangay SitRep

Checked 19 October 2026. This is a barangay-issued BDRRMC Situation Report, adapted to the system's available findings. It is not a DSWD-issued DROMIC publication and does not claim local office approval.

## Applied names

| System finding | Report heading |
| --- | --- |
| Situation overview | Situation Overview |
| Affected population | Status of Affected Areas and Population |
| Damage findings available in the system | Damage Assessment |
| Emergency command-center dispatch activities | Search, Rescue and Retrieval Operations |
| Center capacity, occupancy, vacancy and status | Status of Evacuation Centers |
| Inventory and event-linked requests | Resources Status |

The last two headings describe local operational summaries of government-reporting data categories; they are not claimed as mandatory national section titles. No unrelated sections or national rollups are copied into the barangay report. The prior addendum and annex scheme is superseded.

## Data and local verification

Families and Persons are paired where population is reported. A registered household is the provisional family unit because the registry does not hold an independently validated family census. Missing counts remain Not recorded. Damage findings cover recorded house assessments; no unsupported infrastructure or monetary estimate is invented.

Confirm locally accepted wording and counting conventions with the Cebu City DRRMO and city social welfare office before claiming official-form compliance. No direct office contact or approval was obtained here. The document can be handed over outside the system; no city/regional/national integration is implemented.

Damage output uses recorded minor_damage, major_damage and completely_destroyed assessments, plus scoped detail records. No conversion to total/partial house-damage classifications or monetary estimates is performed. Category counts describe recorded assessments only, not a complete damage census.
